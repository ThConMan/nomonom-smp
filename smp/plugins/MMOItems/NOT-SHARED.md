# MMOItems

## Sorry! I am legally obligated to not share this!

**MMOItems** is a paid plugin. Its config files are the vendor's product, not mine to
hand out, so they are not in this repo.

## What it did on Nomonom

The custom item and gear system. Stat rolls, tiers, and every non-vanilla weapon and armour set players chased. Paired tightly with MythicMobs drops.

## If you want it

[PLUGINS.md](../../../PLUGINS.md). It ships with its own defaults, and tuning
