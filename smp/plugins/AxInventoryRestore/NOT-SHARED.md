# AxInventoryRestore

## Sorry! I am legally obligated to not share this!

**AxInventoryRestore** is a paid plugin. Its config files are the vendor's product, not mine to
hand out, so they are not in this repo.

## What it did on Nomonom

Inventory rollback. The support tool - when someone lost gear to a bug or a bad death, this is what made them whole.
