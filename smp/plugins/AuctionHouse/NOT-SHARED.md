# AuctionHouse

## Sorry! I am legally obligated to not share this!

**AuctionHouse** is a paid plugin. Its config files are the vendor's product, not mine to
hand out, so they are not in this repo.

## What it did on Nomonom

Player-to-player market. The other half of the economy - let players set their own prices instead of everything routing through the admin shop.
