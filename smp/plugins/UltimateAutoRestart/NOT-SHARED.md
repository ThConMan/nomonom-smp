# UltimateAutoRestart

## Sorry! I am legally obligated to not share this!

**UltimateAutoRestart** is a paid plugin. Its config files are the vendor's product, not mine to

## What it did on Nomonom

Scheduled restarts with warnings. Boring, essential, and the reason memory leaks never became outages.
