# CMI

## Sorry! I am legally obligated to not share this!

**CMI** is a paid plugin. Its config files are the vendor's product, not mine to
hand out, so they are not in this repo.

## What it did on Nomonom

The backbone of the whole server. Homes, warps, kits, moderation tools, holograms, playtime rewards, and the glue between economy and permissions. If one plugin defined how Nomonom felt to play on day to day, this was it. Losing it would have meant rebuilding half the server.

## If you want it

Buy it from the vendor. The exact version we ran is in
[PLUGINS.md](../../../PLUGINS.md). It ships with its own defaults, and tuning
from there is the interesting part anyway.
